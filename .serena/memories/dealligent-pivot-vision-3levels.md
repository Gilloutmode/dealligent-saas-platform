## Executive Summary

Dealligent is pivoting to a **3-level architecture** that expands the target from "CI Analysts" to **every employee in a B2B enterprise**.

**Tagline**: "Your company's intelligence, unified and actionable."

---

## Architecture: 3 Levels

### Level 1: INTERNAL SOURCES
- **Target**: Everyone in the organization
- **Promise**: "Ask your company anything."
- **Interface**: 3-column NotebookLLM-style
  - Left: Knowledge Library (Folders & Documents)
  - Center: Conversation (Chat RAG)
  - Right: Intelligence Panel (Agents)
- **Uploads**: Emails, Meetings recordings, Documents (RFP, tech drawings, features)
- **RAG Folders**:
  - Client Cases (tags: client/industry/application/use case)
  - Support Cases (tags: client/industry/application/use case)
  - Product Cases (tags: product/features/application)
  - Technology Cases (tags: product/features/application)

### Level 2: EXTERNAL SOURCES
- **Target**: Strategic decision-makers (VIP)
- **Promise**: "Intelligence on demand."
- **Agents**: @Market, @Product, @Sales, @Technology
- **Sources**: Perplexity AI, Exa, SerpAPI
- **Invocation**:
  - /slash commands (/market, /product)
  - @mentions (@Market, @Product)
  - Sidebar panel (click to invoke)
  - "+" button (enrich response)

### Level 3: MEDIA DIGEST ENGINE
- **Promise**: "Stay ahead, automatically."
- Automated monitoring
- Daily/weekly digests
- Smart alerts
- Continuous learning
